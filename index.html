            How to write a query

            Join terms with & (and) or | (or). Put ! in front of a term to exclude it. Tap an example to use it:

            • p1 … p4: priority
            • today, tomorrow, overdue, no date, next 7 days
            • @label, #Project Name, #Inbox
            • assigned to: me, assigned to: Carol, assigned to: nobody
            • recurring, subtask, search: invoice
